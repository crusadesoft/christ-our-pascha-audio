Listen in a podcast app

The recording is published as a podcast feed. Podcast apps handle offline download, resuming where you left off, and playback speed better than any web page — and you can listen in the car or with the screen off.

Feed address

https://crusadesoft.com/christ-our-pascha-audio/feed.xml

Copy this, then add it in your podcast app as described below.

How to add it

  1. Copy the feed address above.
  2. Open your podcast app and find the option to add a show by URL. It is usually under a + button, or in Search, or in Settings — the exact wording differs by app (see below).
  3. Paste the address and confirm. All 78 parts will appear as episodes, in order, oldest first.
  4. Play from the first episode. Most apps let you download everything for offline listening.
Apple PodcastsLibrary → the … menu → Add a Show by URL. On iPhone, tap Library then the … at the top right.
OvercastThe + at the top right → Add URL at the bottom of the screen.
Pocket CastsProfile → the gear icon → Add Podcast by URL. Also under Discover on some versions.
Podcast Addict / AntennaPodThe + button → Add podcast by RSS / URL.
The feed is not password protected. Podcast apps cannot decrypt the payload this website uses, so anyone given the feed address can subscribe without the password. The episode addresses are not guessable, but please share this address only with reviewers.
